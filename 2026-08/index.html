The evening was given over entirely to information and support for the Perth BitDevs community on the Coldcard seed-generation incident.
This meetup

Coldcard seed generation

Coinkite reported that a firmware bug weakened seed generation on affected Coldcard releases. The devices were not remotely compromised. Installing fixed firmware corrects new seeds; it does not repair a seed already generated on affected firmware.

The session worked through that advisory with people in the community: which seeds were affected, and how to migrate carefully.
