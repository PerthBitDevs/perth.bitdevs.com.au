Newswatch found a cluster of new PQC discussion across Delving Bitcoin and bitcoindev: QCAP, lattice-based signatures, attack-game analysis, Winternitz/Lamport auth chains, P2WOTS, and a wallet-layer hybrid SPHINCS+ / secp256k1 proposal.
QCAP
A Bitcoin-native quantum-canary idea focused on uncertainty around the ECDLP break timeline.
LATTICES

WOTS / LAMPORT
Two Delving threads proposed lightweight Winternitz-style approaches, including a revised P2WOTS design.
WALLET LAYER
A bitcoindev BIP proposal targets hybrid SPHINCS+ / secp256k1 derivation for quantum-resistant paper wallets.

Covenants, Exits, and Replay

Three threads to review together rather than separately

CTV-ONLY VAULT
MCCV v0.1.0 demonstrates a vault concept built using OP_CHECKTEMPLATEVERIFY alone. The author describes deposits, delayed withdrawals, recovery, velocity control, and repeated vault operations, while also flagging computational and usability tradeoffs.
SIBLING-AWARE COVENANTS
A bitcoindev thread maps where CTV and TEMPLATEHASH differ for designs that need sibling prevout commitments, including BitVM, Ark, and LN-Symmetry style constructions.
REPLAY PROTECTION
A Delving thread discusses replay risks in a hypothetical chain split around soft-fork signalling, using "Core" and "Knots" as simplifying labels.
BLOCKSPACE LIMITS
"Credible Exit and The Law of Conservation of Blockspace" argues about limits on trust-minimized layer exits under static block-weight accounting windows. This is a good "read before presenting" candidate.

Lightning Security and Releases

CLN disclosure, release candidates, and routing-economics claims

ASSERTION DOS IN CLN
A Summer of Bitcoin 2025 intern disclosed a remote peer crash in Core Lightning caused by an overly strict assertion in openingd. The issue is reported as patched in Core Lightning v26.04, and operators were advised to upgrade.
CLN v26.06 RCs
RC1 highlights include graceful, sendamount, and xpay taking over pay handling by default. RC2 includes payment-proof draft updates and gossipd robustness work.
LND rc2
LND v0.21.0-beta.rc2 appeared in the scan, along with module releases for queue, kvdb, and sqldb. Needs a deeper changelog read before presenting.
WATCH ITEM
The "Lightning economics" Delving post makes strong claims about routing capital velocity and returns. Treat as a discussion item requiring source review before putting numbers on slides.
