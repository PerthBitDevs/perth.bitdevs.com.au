White Noise Brings Anonymous Messaging to Nostr

Started by Max Hillebrand, former contributor to Wasabi Wallet. Uses MLS (Messaging Layer Security) encryption standard developed by the IETF. Unlike Nostr DMs and public posts today, which leak metadata about who is communicating with whom, White Noise promises to obfuscate sender and recipient using the MLS encryption scheme. MLS provides Forward Secrecy (ephemeral keys ensure past messages remain unreadable if current keys are compromised) and Post-Compromise Security (TreeKEM key rotation protects future messages after a compromise).

Bitcoin Magazine coverage NIP-EE specification Nostr event

80K Old Coins Move

8x 10K BTC transactions moved from older P2PKH addresses to newer P2WPKH (SegWit) addresses. Some speculate it may be "a hack" - Conor Grogan from Coinbase believes the transfer could be the largest hack in history because of a Bitcoin Cash test transaction that preceded the transfers. But others point out: "Given how slowly these sends transpired, it’s hard to be convinced it’s a hack, I’d imagine they would be a bit faster with moving these funds."

Jack Dorsey Launches Bitchat

Bitchat is a decentralized messaging app that runs entirely over Bluetooth mesh networks without internet infrastructure. The system provides encrypted, ephemeral communication with no central servers, accounts, or data storage. Messages hop between devices within 30 meter ranges, making it perfect for conferences, protests, or disaster areas where internet access is unavailable or untrusted.

Samourai Wallet Devs Plead Guilty

Samourai Wallet developers pleaded guilty to conspiring to operate an unlicensed money transmitting business. The conspiracy to commit money laundering charges were dropped as part of the plea deal - they were facing up to 20 years for those charges. Both Rodriguez and Hill face a maximum prison sentence of five years, a $250,000 fine each, and are required to pay a total of $6,367,139.69 between them. They both acknowledged that they knew some bitcoin moving through the service was derived from criminal activity.

Block Open Sources Fee Estimation Library: Augur

Block built Augur, an open-source Bitcoin fee estimator that relies on real-time mempool data instead of historical blocks. Augur uses statistical modeling of historical mempool data to predict transaction confirmation times: it collects and buckets mempool transactions by fee rate, calculates transaction inflow rates at different fee levels, simulates mempool progression based on historical patterns, and produces fee rate estimates with multiple confidence levels.

Utreexo BIPs (x3)

Three new BIPs proposed for Utreexo: the specification of the Utreexo accumulator, the specification of Bitcoin block and transaction validation using the Utreexo accumulator, and the peer-to-peer networking changes required to enable Utreexo nodes. Utreexo is a consensus-compatible (non-soft fork) way to send and verify transactions without storing the full UTXO set.

Simplicity Launches on Liquid Mainnet

Simplicity is a completely new smart contract language designed as a clean-slate vision that’s more expressive than Bitcoin Script. It gives developers much more flexibility but deliberately avoids the complexities and security pitfalls of platforms like Ethereum. By excluding recursion, unbounded loops, and mutable global state, every execution path and fee cost can be proven in advance. Launching on Liquid provides a production environment where real money is at stake, without affecting Bitcoin’s main chain.
